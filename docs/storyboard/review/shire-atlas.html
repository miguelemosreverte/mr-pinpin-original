Create a gorgeous, very cute miniature woodland world seen from a high bird's-eye campaign-map camera like a Total War overworld, but peaceful and intimate for a children's book. About 70 degrees looking downward, no horizon, readable terrain and a consistent miniature scale throughout. Rich dimensional physical textures, finely modeled deciduous tree crowns, firs, fern clearings, mossy rocks, footpaths, meadows of tiny flowers, blue-green water, morning sunlight with restrained highlights, broad crisp depth of field. This is a believable connected landscape, not a diagram and not a floating island.
Input image1 is architectural reference for the PinPin family cottage only: preserve its round green door, two round windows, moss-covered curved roof, left stone chimney and right lantern, miniaturized within the landscape. Input image2 is architectural reference for the Elder's home only: burrow under huge oak roots with a low mossy seat rock nearby. Translate both to the overhead camera.

A winding pedestrian route connects cottage to lake then Elder's hill; a branching path connects cottage and lake to the footbridge. Terrain elevations and water follow believable geography. The Method, in Order

  1. Inspect the existing family cottage and Elder home illustrations.
  2. Direct one new high bird's-eye image with connected water, trails and four readable landmarks.
  3. Save the original-resolution PNG with the exact prompt, references, timestamps and hash beside it.
  4. Inspect the rendered landmark positions and place HTML story markers against the actual image rather than trusting requested coordinates.
  5. Test the atlas at desktop and phone sizes, including sequential opening, persistence, language switching and native gestures.
