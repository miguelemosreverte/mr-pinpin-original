# Scene103 — PinPin washes before lunch

## Exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Standalone1536x1024 warm dimensional children's story illustration. In the EXACT daylight kitchen of reference1, frame a closer view of the LEFT cupboards, camera slightly elevated looking down enough to see washing action. ONE character only: little PinPin from reference2 (small copper-quilled boy with cream muzzle and belly, round ears, black nose, expressive eyes; NOT Mama, Papa or baby). He stands on his TWO own hindfeet on the wooden floor in front of the left cupboards. A wide simple cream ceramic WASH BASIN full of clear water rests securely on ONE low broad wooden stool, rim just below child's waist. PinPin leans slightly forward and dips/rubs his TWO attached forepaws together in water, a few tiny water ripples and drops show actual handwashing. Head tilts down, both eyes gaze at his paws. Exactly four attached limbs, no added human hands. Full child and basin/stool visible, no cut feet. The basin/stool is a REMOVABLE staging prop beside the cupboards, separate from the eating table; do not install faucet, sink/plumbing, or put basin on dining table. Keep cupboard materials/handles, warm wooden wall/floor and daylight consistent with reference1. A small edge of the round dining table and its woven rug may appear on right side as orientation, otherwise close crop naturally excludes door and stove. No mirror/rearranged room. Warm readable midday window light, no dusk. No dog, no other characters, no clothing, glasses, words, labels, panels, floating bowls or duplicated stools. The story beat is child washing dusty paws before family lunch, not eating or splashing bath.
