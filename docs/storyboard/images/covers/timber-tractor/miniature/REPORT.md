## Limits and caveats

This is a visual review, not mechanical certification. Hidden paw attachments, concealed chassis connections and far-side strap anchor points are not verifiable. The distant forest still has fine texture; simplification is mainly through closer framing and subject scale. Some trailer/wheel portions touch the frame edges. Browser thumbnail review is left to the coordinator's comparison report; I have not claimed a measured 96-pixel readability result. User approval remains pending.
