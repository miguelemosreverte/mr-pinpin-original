# Elder revision03 · English draft

Proposed complete story; internally accepted, art review in progress.

## 01. Arrival and study 01

The day after he helped the lake, PinPin went to see the oldest hedgehog on Burrow Hill.

## 02. Arrival and study 02

What would a very old, very wise hedgehog be like?

## 03. Arrival and study 03

There he was, beside a house tucked under the roots of a great tree.

## 04. Arrival and study 04

“Hello, PinPin,” said the Elder, without turning. “I felt you would come today.”

## 05. Arrival and study 05

“You knew? How?”

## 06. Arrival and study 06

“The wind and the leaves bring news,” said the Elder. “I listen.”

## 07. Arrival and study 07

“My golden quill helped the lake,” said PinPin. “Where did its magic come from?” “Let’s look for an answer,” said the Elder.

## 08. Arrival and study 08

On the way to the door, PinPin stopped. “That little plant is stuck.”

## 09. Arrival and study 09

He lifted the stick, carefully, so the leaves could unfold.

## 10. Arrival and study 10

“There. Room to grow,” said PinPin. The Elder smiled.

## 11. Arrival and study 11

“Now come and see where I keep my questions,” said the Elder.

## 12. Arrival and study 12

Inside were books, rolled maps, a soft bed and a little table stained with ink.

## 13. Arrival and study 13

“Do you write books?” “Sometimes. Today I was writing down what I noticed.”

## 14. Arrival and study 14

“Do you know everything?” asked PinPin. “Oh, no,” said the Elder. “That is why I read.”

## 15. Arrival and study 15

A smooth stone. An empty shell. A seed in a little box. “Small things can make us ask big questions,” said the Elder.

## 16. Arrival and study 16

PinPin found the lake on a map. All around it were little homes. “So many neighbours!”

## 17. A page to study

PinPin bent over a picture of leaves. The Elder helped him find the little lines that made each one different.

## 18. Sleepy eyes

The Elder gave a long yawn. “Just a little rest for my eyes,” he murmured.

## 19. A soft snuffle

Soon there was a soft snuffling sound. PinPin looked up. The Elder was asleep.

## 20. A moving page

Flap. One corner of the page lifted, though nobody had touched it.

## 21. A cool smell

PinPin sniffed. Cool air brushed his nose. It smelled of damp earth.

## 22. The sneeze

A speck of dust tickled his nose. “Atchoo!” The Elder did not stir.

## 23. Dust by the mat

Near the roots, a curl of dust moved beside a small mat. PinPin crouched to look.

## 24. Move the little stool

A little footstool was in the way. PinPin slid it aside.

## 25. Roll the small mat

He rolled back the small mat.

## 26. Earth between roots

Between two roots was a dark crack. Cool air came through the loose earth at its edge.

## 27. A few small scrapes

Scrape, scrape. With his paws, PinPin pushed a little loose earth to one side.

## 28. Room for a body

Cool air ruffled his quills. He might just fit.

## 29. First look inside

He lowered his nose. Beyond the opening, pale roots curved into the earth.

## 30. Nose and paws through

First his nose. Then his front paws. PinPin wriggled gently between the roots.

## 31. Quills clear the edge

He tucked himself low. His quills brushed the root, and then he was through.

## 32. Look back at home

PinPin looked back. A little patch of the warm room still shone behind him.

## 33. The sloping passage

The ground sloped gently. He put one paw down, then the next, feeling the earth beneath him.

## 34. Light ahead

Around a pale stone, he saw a thin seam of light. The passage was opening out.

## 35. A small way into a great place

PinPin slipped out between the rocks onto a broad stone floor.

## 36. All alone in the sanctuary

He stood very still. The cave rose high above him. There were pictures on the walls.

## 37. The painted woman

A woman in a long dress looked out from one wall. Beside her were a baby and growing plants.

## 38. A sound behind

Scritch. A little sound came from the passage behind him.

## 39. A small fright

PinPin drew his paws close. “Who’s there?”

## 40. A familiar face

“Only me.” Round spectacles appeared between the roots. It was the Elder.

## 41. Relief

“You surprised me!” said PinPin. “And you surprised me,” said the Elder, with a little smile.

## 42. How he followed

“How did you find me?” “I woke up and saw the moved mat. Then I followed your little paw marks.”

## 43. Who made the pictures

“Who made all this?” “Humans,” said the Elder. “They made this place for their gods.”

## 44. Who tends the lamps

“And the lamps?” “The people who care for this place light them. They look after our forest, too.”

## 45. Why human faces

“Their gods look like them!” “Yes. That is how they painted them.”

## 46. The Mother of Life

PinPin looked at the woman’s round belly. “Is there a baby inside?” “Yes. She is the Mother of Life.”

## 47. A goddess of new life

“Is she a goddess?” “A goddess of fertility. New life: babies being born, and seeds growing into food.”

## 48. Baby and shoots

PinPin found the painted baby. Then the little green shoots. “Both need looking after.”

## 49. Seeds and prayer

“Why are there seeds here?” “People leave them when they pray. They ask the goddess for help, or say thank you.”

## 50. Care at home

“And then?” “Then they go home and care for their babies and gardens.”

## 51. The Shield Keeper

On the next wall was a strong man with a great shield. Little homes stood behind him.

## 52. A god of war

“Who is he?” “The Shield Keeper. A god of war. Look at what he is protecting.”

## 53. What war means

“What is war?” “When large groups of people fight one another. It can hurt people and destroy their homes.”

## 54. What the shield protects

PinPin looked at the little families. “He’s keeping the danger away from them.”

## 55. Safe together

“Yes,” said the Elder. “Here in the picture, he stands between them and harm.” PinPin moved a little closer.

## 56. Strong enough to protect

“Does he like fighting?” “Look behind his shield. He wants those families to be safe.”

## 57. Light against the cold

Another picture showed sunlight, green trees and a great cold darkness. A little house stood between them.

## 58. Night at home

“But I like night,” said PinPin. “Mama tucks me in.”

## 59. Rest and danger

“So do I,” said the Elder. “Night gives us rest. In this picture, the cold storm threatens the little homes.”

## 60. Forest helpers

“They painted animals too!” PinPin found paws carrying wood, bringing food and holding little ones.

## 61. One shield cannot do every job

“Can the Shield Keeper do all those jobs?” “No,” said the Elder. “No one can.”

## 62. Helping one another

“A strong arm can hold a shield. Other paws grow food and build warm homes. They need one another.”

## 63. And my quill

“And my quill?” “Your gift can help. First, look. Listen. Ask who needs you. You do not have to help all alone.”

## 64. Thinking quietly

PinPin thought of the lake, the little plant, and the homes on the map.

## 65. The small opening again

“Back through there?” asked PinPin. “The same way,” said the Elder. “I’m right behind you.”

## 66. Duck beneath the roots

PinPin ducked into the low passage. The Elder bent down to follow.

## 67. Up the gentle slope

They went up slowly. The pale stone was still beside the path.

## 68. A place to pause

At the split root, PinPin paused and looked back. The Elder was there.

## 69. The warm patch

Ahead was the little patch of home. PinPin could smell paper and warm wood again.

## 70. Paws on the home floor

He squeezed through. His front paws found the floor; then came his back feet.

## 71. The Elder comes through

The Elder crouched low and wriggled through after him. A dry leaf clung to his quills.

## 72. A little leaf

“You brought something back,” said PinPin. He lifted the leaf away. They both smiled.

## 73. Tidy the loose earth

Together they brushed the loose earth back beside the roots.

## 74. Unroll the small mat

PinPin unrolled the small mat against the roots. The cool draft softened.

## 75. Put the stool back

They slid the little footstool back. The book was still open at their page.

## 76. Write the question

“What shall I write?” asked the Elder. “How can my magic help?” said PinPin.

## 77. Old magic, more to learn

“Your quill carries old magic from our ancestors,” said the Elder. “There is still much to learn. We will take our time.”

## 78. Outside again

Outside, the leaves glowed gold. The little plant stood free beside the fern.

## 79. Tomorrow at dawn

“Tomorrow at dawn,” said the Elder, “meet me by the oak on the hilltop. We will begin with listening.”

## 80. Bring your questions

“At dawn,” said PinPin. “Thank you.” The Elder smiled. “Bring your questions.”

## 81. The path away

PinPin set off. He had found a hidden place—and so many new questions.
