elder-r6-family-021

elder-r6-family-025

elder-r6-family-027

elder-r6-family-030

elder-r6-family-031

Outside, Scooby drank a little water. Papa and PinPin waited at the garden stone.

elder-r6-family-032

Scooby crouched beside the stone. Papa stepped down to help PinPin.

elder-r6-family-033

From there, PinPin climbed onto Scooby’s back. Papa kept a steady paw beside him.

elder-r6-family-034

“Comfortable?” asked Papa. “Very!” said PinPin. Scooby listened, ready to go.

elder-r6-family-035

Scooby stood up slowly. Mama waved from the door, holding PomPom.

“Can we walk here?” asked PinPin. Papa found a broad stone, and Scooby crouched beside it.

elder-r6-forest-009

elder-r6-forest-012

elder-r6-forest-013

elder-r6-forest-014

elder-r6-forest-015

elder-r6-forest-016

elder-r6-forest-017

Papa put the cup and bowl away. Then they folded the cloth together, leaving the grass clean.

elder-r6-forest-018

elder-r6-forest-019

After a while, PinPin’s steps grew smaller. They stopped together on another broad, low stone.

elder-r6-forest-020

Scooby turned and stayed low. Papa helped PinPin onto his warm back and kept one paw around him.

elder-r6-forest-021

Papa settled behind him. “Comfortable?” “Yes!” Scooby stood up slowly.

elder-r6-forest-022

Then the trees opened. On Burrow Hill stood a great old tree, with a little doorway among its roots.
