# Elder expansion — discussion notes

Status: discussion only. No revised chapter boundaries, numbering, images, or publication are established by this note. The existing revision-05 draft remains the reviewable version.

## Requested direction

- Give the happy, united family enough story for two chapters. Develop affection through shared actions: helping, playing, preparing food, telling stories, and doing things together. More pictures of characters merely looking at one another will not achieve this.
- Give the forest walk and lunch their own chapter, with room for discovery and interaction along the way.
- Frame Papa's decision to visit the Elder as recognizing readiness: a familiar wise man or priest whom the family knows. The visit should feel like a meaningful next step in growing up, rather than an errand to solve a question about magic.
- Extend PinPin's conversation with the Elder. Let him describe what happened at the lake, in his own words, with room for the Elder to listen and respond. The Elder can welcome what he hears and naturally acknowledge that there is magic in this forest.
- Correct continuity at the family table. Everyone's presence, seating, food, and actions must remain understandable across images.

The earlier direction remains relevant: avoid chosen-one declarations and promises of secret training. Preserve the warmth of the family and the mystery of the sanctuary without turning the dialogue into an abstract lecture.

## Placement still to discuss

It is not yet settled whether both family chapters happen before the visit, or whether the second family chapter expands the reunion after it. Do not renumber or rearrange the draft on the assumption that this has been decided.

One provisional five-part structure, if both family chapters precede the visit, is:

1. Papa's arrival and the family coming together.
2. A shared family morning, the lake story, and Papa recognizing readiness.
3. Lunch and exploration in the forest on the way to the Elder.
4. The Elder's home and a fuller conversation about what happened.
5. The roots and sanctuary, followed by a family reunion coda.

These are working descriptions, not approved titles or fixed boundaries. Keeping a substantial family chapter after the visit would change this arrangement. The existing 106 selected images are a reusable pool; chapter lengths should follow complete actions and emotional transitions, with no arbitrary image quotas.
