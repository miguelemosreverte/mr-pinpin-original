# The Elder’s Wisdom: expanded first visit

Status: proposed; official chapter untouched.

## Source and adaptation map

The complete original chapter 2 has 14 prose blocks (0–13). Blocks 0–1 establish Burrow Hill and PinPin's first visit the day after the lake adventure. Blocks 2–4 are the unexpected greeting and listening exchange. Blocks 5–7 introduce power, responsibility and shared silence. Blocks 8–11 preserve the golden quill's unknown origins; the Elder's general reference to ancestors remains his statement, without invented names, histories or flashbacks. Blocks 12–13 promise a meeting at the hilltop tomorrow at dawn and conclude at sunset. That promised lesson is NOT depicted here and remains an unresolved future episode.

Added staging: a pause before approaching, an invitation to sit, listening to an actual leaf, and a small demonstration between blocks 6 and 7. A fallen light twig presses the broad leaves of a tiny seedling beside the path. PinPin first wants to help quickly, then observes and gently lifts the twig with his forepaws. The Elder prompts him to notice what help is needed. This is an ordinary action, not proof of magic or new ancestor lore. The twig is set down safely on bare soil and the leaves rise. No third animal, invented interior, tools, furniture or magic effects.
