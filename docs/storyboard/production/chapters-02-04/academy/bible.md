## Site and axis lock
Single outdoor woodland school garden. Overview looking north: low open-sided timber pavilion in far-left/northwest (no enclosed interior ever shown), moss roof on four posts, low log benches beneath. Central low circular stump is teacher perch; broad lawn south gives space for three children. Old oak east/right with three root hollows facing southwest; daisies beside its south root. Small shallow stream west/left, separated by firm dry bank; smooth boulder landmark at bend. Snag for woodpecker north/center beside path, no cavity house. A curved walking path runs pavilion → central clearing → stream left → snag north → central clearing; no bridge, fence, road, river crossing. Homeward path exits oak clearing at southeast/right foreground. Camera generally stays southwest facing northeast, consistent left stream / right oak. Travel reverses explicitly with establishing wide shots; closer shots preserve scene eyelines. No invented interiors.

Scale: characters small woodland animals; teacher perch about PinPin shoulder height; stream only a few animal body lengths across, shallow with pebbles; feet stay on dry ground. Oak trunk at least four adult owl body widths. Children listen together without handling wildlife. No instruments; forest sounds are real, no glowing music symbols.
