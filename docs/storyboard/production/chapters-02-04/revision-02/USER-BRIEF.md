## Execution interpretation

Implement this direction without asking Miguel to restate the religious themes. Use the source's own mythic light/winter conflict and communal preparation as the story connection. Explicitly depict and explain the requested fertility/motherhood/pregnancy and male defender/war-deity archetypes through gentle symbolic murals and age-four dialogue; do not replace the religion concept with only a generic kindness lesson. The historical analogy is Miguel's thematic direction, not evidence that named Soviet history occurs in the original forest story. Read the entire book and preserve later reveals.

Design the continuous exterior → tree dwelling → interior door → safe descent → cave plateau/murals → ascent → dwelling → exterior geography before final scene renders. Practical lighting, furnishings, books/writing/artifacts, bodily scale and partner eyelines are required production details. Warm mentor, curious young child, pictorial discovery and short exchanges; no long abstract lecture.
