## What is inside

The Elder chapter develops a complete visit: a lived-in tree home, reading and writing, artifacts and maps, a practical inner door and stairs, a naturally lit mural cavern, and the full return route. The conversation explicitly covers religion, fertility and new life, the male defender and god of war, light and destructive darkness, and the different kinds of work a community needs. Short child questions accompany concrete pictures. Original magic, ancestry and next-dawn threads remain without revealing later plot answers.

School begins with Mama, PinPin and baby PomPom walking through morning mist and dew. The Elder welcomes five pupils with playful pinecone pretend-play. Mouse Mila and badger Bruno join Lulu and Tutu, each contributing to listening, building and testing a miniature shelter. The crown sequence is followed by family reunions, Mama greeting another parent, everyone seeing the children's work, and a shared daylight walk.
