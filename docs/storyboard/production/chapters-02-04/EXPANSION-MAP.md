# What changed in the two chapter proposals

## The Elder’s Wisdom

The existing sixteen story images remain selected where they fit. Fourteen new images expand the first visit to thirty story beats, plus its existing title cover. This is a proposed new reading sequence and new trilingual copy; the original reader/source remains intact.

| Existing coverage | Expanded sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Journey → immediate greeting | Journey, first sight, hesitation, step into clearing, unexpected greeting and reaction. |
| Canopy illustration with a line about listening | Canopy, PinPin actively listening, invitation and settling into a seat. |
| One explanation about power and responsibility | Explanation, notice a trapped plant, inspect obstacle, reach too quickly, pause, look closely, lift, set aside, observe recovery, discuss what helped, return to seats. |
| Conversation about the quill and future lesson | Keeps original strong portraits, simplifies dense copy, preserves the unanswered origin and dawn invitation. |
| Sunset departure and silent peek | Adds goodbye before departure; retains the two separate silent closing images. |

New practical demonstration is adaptation material, not an event claimed from the source. It gives responsibility a visible meaning without inventing ancient lore or new magic powers.

## The Forest Academy

The original has one seven-beat after-school scene repeated in slightly different prose. It is adapted once. The new proposal adds one school activity before it, expanding to thirty-six story images plus a new localized title cover.

| Original beat or proposed addition | Expanded sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Added ordinary academy afternoon | Gather with proposed owl teacher, listen to leaves, notice water, walk to stream, settle on dry bank, discover tapping, approach and observe woodpecker, return and end lesson. |
| Friends sit beneath old oak | Walk over, choose root seats, settle as a trio. |
| Tutu tells a honey-jar anecdote | Invitation to listen, an orally told concrete joke, laughter/reaction. No unexplained raccoon flashback. |
| Lulu makes a flower crown | Gather daisies, handle stems, extend chain, join ring, put it on; crown stays with Lulu. |
| Forest listening and belonging | Quiet attention and shared expressions, grounded in sounds from earlier activity. |
| Mama calls and they go home | Hear call, establish her arrival, move from seat, greet, say goodbye, walk together, reach familiar home. |

Academy takes place on another unspecified day. It does not depict or cancel the Elder's promised dawn follow-up. The original chronology thread remains recorded in `world-state.json` for a future episode.
