## Morning

### 001 — new

The morning after PinPin helped the lake, mist lay low over the garden. Someone was coming along the path.

### 002 — new

It was Papa, riding Scooby! The little brown dog stopped beside the broad garden stone.

### 003 — new

Scooby crouched. Papa stepped onto the stone, then down onto the ground.

### 004 — new

PinPin pressed his nose to the window. “Papa!”

### 005 — new

Mama opened the door with PomPom in her arms. PinPin hurried into Papa’s hug. Scooby’s tail curled up.

## Breakfast

### 006 — new

Soon they were all around the breakfast table. Papa poured tea. Mama held PomPom close.

### 007 — new

“Yesterday, we helped the lake,” PinPin told Papa. “The water came back!”

### 008 — new

“And my golden quill helped,” he added. Papa put down his cup and listened.

### 009 — new

“I know an old friend who listens very carefully,” said Papa. “The Elder. Shall we visit him together?”

### 010 — new

“Yes, please!” said PinPin. Mama smiled. “Tell him hello from us. We’ll have lunch together when you’re home.”

## Outward Boarding

### 011 — new

Outside, Scooby drank a little water. Papa and PinPin waited at the garden stone.

### 012 — new

Scooby crouched beside the stone. Papa stepped down to help PinPin.

### 013 — new

From there, PinPin climbed onto Scooby’s back. Papa kept a steady paw beside him.

### 014 — new

“Comfortable?” asked Papa. “Very!” said PinPin. Scooby listened, ready to go.

### 015 — new

Scooby stood up slowly. Mama waved from the door, with PomPom against her shoulder.

## Journey

### 016 — new

They went at a gentle walking pace. PinPin felt Scooby’s warm back moving beneath him.

### 017 — new

The mist thinned between the trees. Ahead, a great tree spread its roots across Burrow Hill.

## Arrival

### 018 — new

Scooby stopped beside a flat stone near the Elder’s house and crouched again.

### 019 — new

Papa stepped down onto the stone first. Scooby stayed perfectly still.

### 020 — new

Then Papa helped PinPin down. His little feet found the stone, and then the path.

### 021 — new

“Good morning, old friend!” Papa called. The Elder came to meet them, smiling behind his round spectacles.

### 022 — new

“PinPin has a story about the lake—and a few questions.” “Then we have plenty to talk about,” said the Elder.

## Outside Rest

### 023 — new

A warm patch of sunshine lay beside the roots. Scooby curled up there, and Papa sat down beside him.

### 024 — new

“I’ll be right here,” Papa told PinPin. “Enjoy your visit.” Scooby gave a long, sleepy sigh.

### 025 — new

By the time PinPin turned toward the door, Papa’s eyes were closing too.

## Garden Pause

### 026 — reuse

On the way to the door, PinPin stopped. “That little plant is stuck.”

### 027 — reuse

He lifted the stick, carefully, so the leaves could unfold.

### 028 — reuse

“There. Room to grow,” said PinPin. The Elder smiled.

## Study

### 029 — reuse

“Now come and see where I keep my questions,” said the Elder.

### 030 — reuse

Inside were books, rolled maps, a soft bed and a little table stained with ink.

### 031 — reuse

“Do you write books?” “Sometimes. Today I was writing down what I noticed.”

### 032 — reuse

“Do you know everything?” asked PinPin. “Oh, no,” said the Elder. “That is why I read.”

### 033 — reuse

A smooth stone. An empty shell. A seed in a little box. “Small things can make us ask big questions,” said the Elder.

### 034 — reuse

PinPin found the lake on a map. All around it were little homes. “So many neighbours!”

### 035 — reuse

“What do these little marks say?” asked PinPin. The Elder read the leaf names aloud. PinPin listened closely.

### 036 — reuse

The Elder gave a long yawn. “Just a little rest for my eyes,” he murmured.

### 037 — reuse

Soon there was a soft snuffling sound. PinPin looked up. The Elder was asleep.

## Root Discovery

### 038 — reuse

Flap. One corner of the page lifted, though nobody had touched it.

### 039 — reuse

PinPin sniffed. Cool air brushed his nose. It smelled of damp earth.

### 040 — reuse

A speck of dust tickled his nose. “Atchoo!” The Elder did not stir.

### 041 — reuse

Near the roots, a curl of dust moved beside a small mat. PinPin crouched to look.

### 042 — reuse

A little footstool was in the way. PinPin slid it aside.

### 043 — reuse

He rolled back the small mat.

### 044 — reuse

Between two roots was a dark crack. Cool air came through the loose earth at its edge.

### 045 — reuse

Scrape, scrape. With his paws, PinPin pushed a little loose earth to one side.

### 046 — reuse

Cool air ruffled his quills. He might just fit.

### 047 — reuse

He lowered his nose. Beyond the opening, pale roots curved into the earth.

### 048 — reuse

First his nose. Then his front paws. PinPin wriggled gently between the roots.

### 049 — reuse

He tucked himself low. His quills brushed the root, and then he was through.

### 050 — reuse

PinPin looked back. A little patch of the warm room still shone behind him.

### 051 — reuse

The ground sloped gently. He put one paw down, then the next, feeling the earth beneath him.

### 052 — reuse

Around a pale stone, he saw a thin seam of light. The passage was opening out.

## Sanctuary Discovery

### 053 — reuse

PinPin slipped out between the rocks onto a broad stone floor.

### 054 — reuse

He stood very still. The cave rose high above him. There were pictures on the walls.

### 055 — reuse

A woman in a long dress looked out from one wall. Beside her were a baby and growing plants.

### 056 — reuse

Scritch. A little sound came from the passage behind him.

### 057 — reuse

PinPin drew his paws close. “Who’s there?”

### 058 — reuse

“Only me.” Round spectacles appeared between the roots. It was the Elder.

### 059 — reuse

“You surprised me!” said PinPin. “And you surprised me,” said the Elder, with a little smile.

### 060 — reuse

“How did you find me?” “I woke up and saw the moved mat. Then I followed your little paw marks.”

## Sanctuary Stories

### 061 — reuse

“Who made this place?” “Humans,” said the Elder. “They painted these stories long ago. Some still come here.”

### 062 — reuse

“Who lights the lamps?” “People who care for this place. They help protect our forest, too.”

### 063 — reuse

“The painted people look like them!” “Yes,” said the Elder. “They gave the pictures faces like their own.”

### 064 — reuse

PinPin looked at the woman’s round belly. “Is there a baby inside?” “Yes. They call her the Mother of Life.”

### 065 — reuse

“Like PomPom, before he was born?” “Just like that,” said the Elder. “And look beside her—little seeds are growing.”

### 066 — reuse

PinPin found the painted baby. Then the little green shoots. “Both need looking after.”

### 067 — reuse

“Why are there real seeds here?” “Someone left them with a wish, or a thank-you,” said the Elder, softly.

### 068 — reuse

“And then?” “Then they went home to care for their babies and gardens.”

### 069 — reuse

On the next wall was a strong man with a great shield. Little homes stood behind him.

### 070 — reuse

“Who is he?” “The Shield Keeper. Look behind his shield.”

### 071 — reuse

PinPin found the little families. “He’s keeping the danger away from them.”

### 072 — reuse

“Yes,” said the Elder. “He stands between them and harm.” PinPin moved a little closer.

### 073 — reuse

Another picture showed sunlight, green trees and a great cold darkness. A little house stood between them.

### 074 — reuse

“But I like night,” said PinPin. “Mama tucks me in.”

### 075 — reuse

“So do I,” said the Elder. “Night gives us rest. In this picture, the cold storm threatens the little homes.”

### 076 — reuse

“They painted animals too!” PinPin found paws carrying wood, bringing food and holding little ones.

### 077 — reuse

“Can the Shield Keeper do all those jobs?” “No,” said the Elder. “No one can.”

### 078 — reuse

“Some carry wood. Some bring food. Some hold the little ones,” said the Elder. “They help one another.”

### 079 — reuse

PinPin looked once more at the mother, the shield, and the little homes. There was so much to remember.
