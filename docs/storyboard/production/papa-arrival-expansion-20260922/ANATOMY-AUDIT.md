Reviewed 2026-09-22. Scope: the selected outputs for arrival-04, 05, 06, 07,
08, 10, 11 and 12 in `generation-plan.json`, plus retained published
`elder-r6-family-005.webp` through `008.webp`. Findings are from opening the
actual images, not from assuming the prompts were followed. Window/glass
continuity is covered separately by the coordinator.

## Required repair

**Arrival-10 (`images/arrival-10.png`): infant merges into Mama.** At Mama's
upper chest/near side, PomPom reads as a head and tiny forearm emerging from her
body. His quills merge directly into her large quilled silhouette; his torso,
bottom and legs cannot be followed as an independent body. Mama's forward
gesture is readable, but there is no clearly traceable carrying forearm beneath
the infant. This is more than an ordinary hidden limb: the image does not
establish the baby's separate volume or how he is being carried.

Keep the walking composition and expressions. Redraw only the infant/carrying
area with an independent small oval torso, bent legs and visible feet; a
rounded baby-quill contour in front of Mama; and Mama's forearm beneath his
bottom with her paw around his far side. A narrow contact shadow should
separate their bodies. No additional maternal arm, carrier, or clothing is
needed. Arrival-06 and arrival-07 are stronger support references than the
defective scene itself.

## Acceptance check for replacements

Open each replacement at full scene size and inspect the carrying area closely.
Confirm separate infant head/torso/back, traceable maternal shoulder-to-elbow-to-
hand anatomy, visible support under the baby's bottom, and no extra arms or
feet. Preserve scale, identity, connected eyelines and the approved warm style.
Compare before/after in the review page before replacing any official image.
