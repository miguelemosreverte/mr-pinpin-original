# Focused wall-join repair

One built-in image-model call repaired the extracted 110° perspective view. The
model reconstructed the interrupted shelf, wall, timber and hanging foliage
without adding a vertical object to conceal the join. The original generated
1254-square repair is preserved separately from the composite panorama.

The synchronized browser review confirms that the conspicuous wall split is
removed in the combined sphere. Reviewed left and right blend boundaries show
no obvious doubled door/window contours. The output remains a proposal, with
small local redrawing rather than exact geometry preservation.

This is **not a fully seamless room**. The untouched upper and lower regions
retain the native panorama's distorted ceiling beams, rug and foliage. Their
transitions are visible when looking toward the poles. The prior bathroom and
stove identity errors remain as well. Repairing the wall alone did not bring
the complete experience up to the stronger historical versions.

The composite changes 225,568 pixels (14.335% of the panorama). Decoded pixels
outside the analytic mask, the opposite meridian, and polar caps at latitude
55° and beyond remain identical. The ±40° wall-band boundary difference drops
from mean 42.991 to 5.988 on the 0–255 channel scale; this supports the local
result but cannot certify visual continuity or the whole sphere.
