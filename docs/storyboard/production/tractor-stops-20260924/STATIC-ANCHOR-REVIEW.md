# Rectangular source-frame anchor

The isolated renderer `docs/tractor-panorama-anchor.js` projects the original raw video frame into its registered 16:9 perspective frustum, then samples it by world ray as the viewer turns. Generated scenery remains outside the known field. No model, pixel painting or geometry reconstruction is involved. The shared panorama renderer and main UI were not modified by this prototype.

The constructor accepts `{yaw,pitch,fov,aspect,featherStart}` for the source camera. Entry camera must equal that descriptor; it is not always 65°. The default prototype uses yaw0/pitch0/VFOV65, while `tractor-static-review.html?camera=fitted` uses the new04 candidate's measured descriptor65.5083°. `featherStart:.94` gives full source weight until the outer3% of each frame edge, then smoothly fades to generated scenery. The complete tractor lies inside the full-weight region. This is a rectangular projection, not a stretched square texture.

Actual Chrome/WebGL captures on the mini cover generated/anchored entry, ±30° and ±60° turns, plus mobile390. No JavaScript errors occurred. At a1280×720 entry, the full-weight interior rectangle `[39,22,1241,698]` compared against the original canvas pixels has **maximum RGB error1**, mean **0.004549/255**, and **zero channels above1**, across2,437,656 channels. Both authored65° and fitted65.5083° tests give these results. The one-level error is bilinear/float sampling roundoff. This is not a claim of byte-identical export or known geometry outside the source field.

The source tractor and original blur remain stable during head rotation. Actual30°/60° views expose a narrow scenery transition: the blurred source foreground/trees differ from generated terrain. Thus rigid source geometry passes, while the boundary is not yet visually seamless. Root accepted the control mechanism and requested a separate one-call background-join repair; any such repair must remain beneath the unchanged source layer.

Evidence remains on the mini under `browser-check/static-anchor/` and `browser-check/static-anchor-fitted/`: `anchor-0.png`, `anchor-30.png`, `anchor-60.png`, corresponding generated and negative-yaw captures, `mobile.png`, and `results.json`. Capture scripts are `browser-check-static-anchor.cjs` and `browser-check-static-anchor-fitted.cjs`. The local preview is `/tractor-static-review.html`; original main viewer behavior is unchanged by these three isolated files.
