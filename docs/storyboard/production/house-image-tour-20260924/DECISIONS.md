# Image-led room tour: decisions

Recorded 2026-09-24 UTC during the user's requested experiment.

- Objective: bootstrap adjoining rooms from the preferred historical A panorama, with usable door transitions and no new Blender work. The ancestor A still has Blender-derived geometry; this is not a claim that the whole lineage was created without Blender.
- Preserve the approved-looking common-room base and its front/up/down repair textures. Add two interior openings by editing only its original rear perspective image. This limits changes to the part of the room that needs new doors.
- Generate each private room as one complete 2:1 spherical image. Give the model the fully repaired A panorama, the new common-room doorway image, and that room's actual book illustration. These three inputs respectively supply projection/appearance, neighboring-room context, and room-specific prop design.
- Inspect ordinary perspective projections before deciding what to repair. Both new ceilings were coherent enough to retain. Both floors pinched near their rugs, and both panorama wraps had a visible rear-wall join. The bathroom return door also used the wrong side for its leaf.
- Make five focused image edits: bathroom front/rear/down, bedroom rear/down. Keep each as a separate source-resolution texture in the existing spherical renderer. This first set used eight model calls. A later local cleanup brings the final total to nine generated masters; no six-face generation and no new Blender render were used.
- Register hotspots against the actual painted apertures. The model did not put both private doors at the requested exact center longitude, so configuration records their observed positions instead of pretending the prompt was obeyed precisely.
- Keep the bath front repair's full leaf visible through the feather transition: the generic repair feather left a faint part of the original leaf. A bounded deterministic projection of the SAME generated correction into a new base provides coverage; no additional artistic regeneration is implied. Record the exact mask and derivative separately. The same registered front correction must also be projected into the overlapping down repair, because that older floor image still contains the removed left-hand door leaf. Both derivatives reuse generated pixels; those two operations are projection/compositing. Inspection then found that a small lower fragment lay outside the front image’s angular coverage. One final local image edit, `bath/down-v3.png`, removes that fragment from the composed down view; this is the ninth model call.
- Interpret this result as a connected illustrated tour with three fixed viewing points. The matching motifs and door directions are visual continuity evidence, not proof of surveyed dimensions or continuous camera translation.

Every generation has its exact prompt and input/output hashes beside its room assets. The originals remain on TB4 and are preserved through the scoped asset archive. The historical panorama, book images and canonical measured location pack are unchanged.
